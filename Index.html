Welcome to Paradise

A pristine 5-hectare island with powdery white sandbar stretching into crystal waters.

Destination Overview

Kalanggaman Island is located in Palompon, Leyte. Known for its iconic long sandbar that disappears during high tide, it offers untouched natural beauty and vibrant marine life.

Why Visit Kalanggaman?

  • Longest white sandbar in the Philippines
  • Crystal clear water perfect for snorkeling
  • Stargazing and bioluminescent plankton at night
  • Local hospitality from Palompon community

Fast Facts

  • Location: Palompon, Leyte, Philippines
  • Best Season: December to May
  • Boat Time: 45-60 mins from Palompon port
  • Entry: Environmental fee + boat rental
